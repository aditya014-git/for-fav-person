Put your own pictures in this folder and use these exact filenames:
cover.jpg                 - first page framed picture
pookie-cat-flower.png     - cat holding a flower (transparent PNG looks nice)
birthday-cat.png          - cat wearing a birthday hat
theatre.jpg               - her picture shown on the theatre screen
rose-garden.jpg           - rose garden picture
photo1.jpg                - gallery / falling photo 1
photo2.jpg                - gallery / falling photo 2
photo3.jpg                - gallery / falling photo 3
photo4.jpg                - gallery / falling photo 4
portrait.jpg              - picture for the long birthday message

You can use JPG, PNG or WEBP, but if you use another extension, also change the filename in templates/index.html and static/js/script.js.
